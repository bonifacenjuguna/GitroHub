🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What We Collect

Telegram identity (user ID, username, name), an encrypted GitHub OAuth token, sensitive-action logs, and your bot preferences.

What We Do NOT Collect

Your GitHub password, permanent copies of your source code, or any data sold/shared with third parties.

How Data Is Secured

GitHub tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a per-account derived key. Secrets sent in chat are deleted immediately after being read. Destructive actions require explicit confirmation.

Data Retention

Preferences and logs are retained until you delete your GitroHub account, at which point everything is permanently erased. Session/cache data in Redis is ephemeral.

Your Rights

You may delete your GitroHub account and all associated data at any time directly within the bot.

Third-Party Services

GitroHub depends on the GitHub API, the Telegram Bot API, and Railway (hosting, PostgreSQL, Redis), each governed by its own privacy policy.
