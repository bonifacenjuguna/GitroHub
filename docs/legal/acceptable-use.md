# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: July 31, 2026**

GitroHub may not be used to:

- Access repositories or resources you do not have rights to.
- Automate abuse of the GitHub API, including spam or mass scraping.
- Upload malware, illegal content, or content that infringes on the
  rights of others.
- Circumvent GitHub's own Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policies.
- Attempt to bypass GitroHub's owner-only access control.

Since this is a private, single-owner bot instance, this policy exists
primarily to document expected use rather than to govern a multi-tenant
user base — but it applies in full to any authorized use of the Software.

Violation of this policy may result in immediate revocation of access,
consistent with the termination terms in [`LICENSE.md`](../../LICENSE.md).
